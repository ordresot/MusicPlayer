<p>VoidPlayer is a premium, open-source local music player for Android, crafted with Kotlin Multiplatform and Jetpack Compose.</p>

<p>It is designed with a heavy emphasis on modern UI aesthetics, dynamic colors, and buttery smooth performance, without any ads, trackers, or hidden analytics.</p>

<p><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
  <li>Completely Free and Open Source (FOSS)</li>
  <li>Gorgeous UI with Dynamic Colors extracted from your album art</li>
  <li>Dynamic Island Player Overlay (access your music from anywhere)</li>
  <li>Media3 ExoPlayer Engine for high-fidelity audio</li>
  <li>Battery-efficient local file scanning</li>
  <li>Built-in Equalizer</li>
  <li>No telemetry, no ads, completely private</li>
</ul>
