VoidPlayer is a premium, open-source local music player for Android, crafted with Kotlin Multiplatform and Jetpack Compose. 

It is designed with a heavy emphasis on modern UI aesthetics, dynamic colors, and buttery smooth performance, without any ads, trackers, or hidden analytics.

Features:
- Completely Free and Open Source (FOSS)
- Gorgeous UI with Dynamic Colors extracted from your album art
- Dynamic Island Player Overlay (access your music from anywhere)
- Media3 ExoPlayer Engine for high-fidelity audio
- Battery-efficient local file scanning
- Built-in Equalizer
- No telemetry, no ads, completely private
